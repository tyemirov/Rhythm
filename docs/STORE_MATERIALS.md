## Description

Free · Built by Marco Polo Research Lab

Flow gives your work a place in the macOS menu bar.
Start a Wave, work at your own pace, and take a Pause when you need one.
Leave a resume note before a Pause to keep your next step in view.

Flow suggests a Pause after 60 minutes.
At 90 minutes, a separate application window reminds you to take a Pause.
You can defer a Pause once for five minutes before the 120-minute limit.
At 120 minutes, Flow shows an extended work reminder.
You control when work stops and when the next Wave starts.

Optional chimes use your current audio output.
Notification delivery follows your macOS settings.
Focus remains under your control in Control Center.
Flow does not change Focus automatically.

Notes, preferences, and History stay on your Mac.
Flow has no account, analytics, or cloud synchronization.

Explore other Marco Polo Research Lab projects from the lab page.
Project links open in your default browser.
Flow sends no notes, History, or preferences with these links.

## Review Notes

1. Open Flow.
2. Find the wave icon in the menu bar.
3. Click the icon if the first popover is closed.
4. Click Start wave to start a Wave.
5. Click Pause to prepare a resume note.
6. Enter a note.
7. Click Begin pause to start a Pause.
8. Select the play control, Continue, to continue the same Wave.
9. Open Settings to find the notification and chime controls.
10. Click Quit Flow in Settings to close the application.

Flow has no Dock window or account login.
The first launch opens its popover.
The production timer uses real elapsed time.
The 60-minute notification needs notification permission.
The 90-minute and 120-minute application windows do not need notification permission.

The microphone button shows macOS Dictation instructions.
It does not record audio.
Manual Focus instructions appear in Settings.
